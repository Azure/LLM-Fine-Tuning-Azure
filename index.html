What it is

Retraining models for your specific tasks

Supervised Fine-Tuning takes an existing pre-trained language model and retrains it using your own example data. The result is a new “custom” model — one that understands your domain, speaks in your tone, and performs with the accuracy your use case demands.

Think of it as the difference between hiring a generalist and an expert. The base model has broad knowledge; fine-tuning sharpens it into a specialist.

Why use it

Go beyond prompting

When prompt engineering and RAG have reached their limits, fine-tuning unlocks the next level of model performance.

🎯

Task-specific accuracy

Dramatically improve performance on narrow tasks where the base model falls short — from domain Q&A to structured data extraction.

✍️

Consistent style & tone

Steer outputs to match your brand voice, clinical terminology, or legal language without elaborate system prompts.

⚡

Lower latency at scale

Replace long, complex prompts with learned behavior. Shorter inputs mean faster inference and reduced token costs in production.

📚

Encode rare knowledge

Introduce information that the base model never learned well — proprietary processes, niche domains, or evolving regulations.
